# Brand Guidelines

## Brand position

K9 Community is a warm, launch-ready dog community brand focused on local belonging, trusted support, and visible impact for dogs and the humans who care for them.

## Audience

- Dog guardians and families
- Rescue adopters and foster volunteers
- Trainers and behavior professionals
- Local shelters, rescues, and pet-friendly businesses

## Voice

- Warm, optimistic, and confident
- Community-first rather than corporate
- Helpful and action-oriented
- Specific about value, impact, and next steps

## Messaging pillars

1. **Belonging** — dogs and people should feel connected to a real local network
2. **Support** — training, rescue response, and practical guidance should be easy to access
3. **Action** — membership, volunteering, and partnership should lead to visible outcomes

## Visual direction

### Core palette

- Accent purple: `#7c3aed`
- Heading ink: `#111827`
- Page background: `#f8fafc`
- Surface: `#ffffff`
- Warm highlight: `#fef3c7`

### UI cues

- Rounded cards and buttons to keep the experience approachable
- Bright accent CTAs for membership and partnership actions
- Soft gradients and shadows to avoid a sterile “template” feel

## Photography and imagery

Use photography that feels:

- local rather than stock-heavy
- outdoors, social, and community-centered
- inclusive across breeds, ages, and family types
- reflective of training, adoption, volunteering, and neighborhood events

## CTA hierarchy

- Primary CTA: join the membership / start the launch conversation
- Secondary CTA: contact the team / explore events / partnership outreach

## Copy do / don’t

### Do

- say what the member gets
- explain the community impact
- ground claims in recognizable numbers or outcomes

### Don’t

- use generic “best-in-class” filler
- overpromise with unsupported rescue or revenue claims
- sound like a temporary MVP or placeholder project
